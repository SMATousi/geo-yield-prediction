Generated by `yieldsat_paper_compare.py`. Values are R² / RMSE (t/ha) computed on the
pooled out-of-fold predictions of all folds (the paper's computation; see
`results/negative_r2_investigation.md`); ± is the std over seeds where several seeds
ran. Paper rows show the paper's values and come from arXiv:2604.00940 Tables
13–18; `*` marks cells where the PDF and the project results page disagree.

like-for-like comparisons. "re-run" rows are our re-implementations of the paper's
baselines (PC-05); "ours" rows are our models.
